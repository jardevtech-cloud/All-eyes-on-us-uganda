Our Approach

Every Young Person Deserves an Opportunity

All Eyes on Us Uganda uses creativity, performance, teamwork and community participation to create positive opportunities for children and young people.

Our programmes provide spaces where participants can explore their interests, practise their abilities, build confidence and learn from one another.

Discover What We Do

Programmes That Develop Talent and Potential

Explore the different areas through which we encourage creativity, learning, discipline and community involvement.

PROGRAMME 01

Dance and Cultural Expression

We encourage young people to express themselves through dance, movement and cultural performance. Dance provides an opportunity to develop creativity, confidence and teamwork.

  • Dance practice and performance
  • Cultural expression and creativity
  • Teamwork and coordination
  • Participation in community showcases
Creativity • Culture • Confidence
PROGRAMME 02

Acrobatics

Our acrobatics activities encourage physical expression, coordination, discipline and cooperation through supervised practice and performance.

  • Coordination and body awareness
  • Teamwork and cooperation
  • Practice and performance preparation
  • Confidence and discipline
Discipline • Teamwork • Skill
PROGRAMME 03

Music and Brass Band

Through music and band activities, young people can explore musical expression, develop discipline and learn how to work together as a group.

  • Music and rhythm practice
  • Group coordination
  • Performance preparation
  • Participation in community events
Music • Expression • Cooperation
PROGRAMME 04

Scouting and Community Participation

We value activities that encourage young people to become responsible, active and helpful members of their communities.

  • Teamwork and leadership development
  • Community participation
  • Responsibility and service
  • Outdoor and group activities
Service • Responsibility • Unity
Programme 05

Education and Learning Support

We recognise the importance of education in helping children and young people build a better future. Our education support activities aim to encourage learning and connect young people with opportunities.

  • Encouraging participation in education
  • Learning and personal development activities
  • Connecting young people with opportunities
  • Community support for learning
