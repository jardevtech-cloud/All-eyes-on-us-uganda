Founder

Luwaga Moses

Founder – All Eyes on Us Uganda

Luwaga Moses is the founder of All Eyes on Us Uganda – Kalleous Organization Ltd. His vision helped establish an organization focused on bringing people together through music, talent, culture and community.

The organization continues to build its identity around participation, creativity and opportunities for people to contribute their talents and ideas.
