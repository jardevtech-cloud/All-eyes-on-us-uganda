WHO WE ARE

Turning Talent Into Opportunity. Transforming Lives Through Creativity.

All Eyes on Us Uganda – Kalleous Organization Ltd. is a community-driven organization committed to empowering vulnerable children and young people.

Through dance, acrobatics, music, cultural activities, scouting and education support, we create opportunities for young people to discover their abilities and develop their potential.

We believe that talent can become a pathway to confidence, personal development, meaningful participation and a better future.

Our Belief

Every child deserves a chance to be seen, heard, supported and given an opportunity to shine.

OUR JOURNEY

Growing Through Creativity and Community.

Our journey is built around bringing children and young people together, nurturing their abilities and creating opportunities for them to participate in community life.

2016 OUR ESTABLISHMENT

A Journey of Purpose

Since 2016, All Eyes on Us Uganda has been committed to using creativity, talent and community participation as tools for positive change.

Our work focuses on creating an environment where children and young people can learn, express themselves, develop confidence and discover their potential.

We continue to build relationships with individuals, schools, organizations and partners who share our vision.
