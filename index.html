WHO WE ARE

All Eyes on Us Uganda

All Eyes on Us Uganda – Kalleous Organization Ltd. is a community-driven organization committed to empowering vulnerable children and young people through creative expression, music, cultural activities and talent development.

We believe that every child deserves an opportunity to discover their abilities, develop confidence and participate meaningfully in their community.

Learn About Us →

Our Mission

To empower vulnerable children and young people by nurturing their talents, developing practical skills and creating opportunities through dance, acrobatics, music and cultural expression.

Our Vision

A society where every child and young person has the opportunity to discover their potential and build a better future through creativity and opportunity.
