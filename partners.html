Let's Create Opportunities That Matter

All Eyes on Us Uganda – Kalleous Organization Ltd. believes that lasting community development is strengthened when people and organisations work together.

We welcome partnerships with institutions, schools, businesses, foundations, community groups and individuals who share our interest in supporting children and young people.

We begin by understanding your organisation's goals and identifying practical areas where our work and your interests can complement one another.

Ways to Collaborate

Partnership Opportunities

There are different ways to work together. Each partnership can be discussed and shaped around shared objectives.

01

Education and Learning Support

Collaborate on educational activities, learning resources and opportunities that help young people develop.

02

Talent and Creative Development

Support dance, acrobatics, music, cultural expression and other creative activities.

03

Equipment and Resources

Explore contributions of suitable equipment, materials and resources needed for programme activities.

04

Skills Sharing and Mentorship

Connect young people with professionals, mentors, trainers and people willing to share their experience.

05

Community Outreach

Work together on activities that encourage participation, inclusion and community engagement.

06

Long-Term Institutional Partnerships

Develop ongoing relationships around shared priorities, agreed activities and measurable objectives.

Getting Started

How Partnership Discussions Work

We want to understand your interests before proposing a suitable way forward.

1

Start a Conversation

2

Identify Shared Goals

3

Agree on a Way Forward

Together, we identify practical next steps, responsibilities and an appropriate collaboration plan.
